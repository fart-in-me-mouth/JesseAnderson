The work

A systematic defense of a natural life

The Apexist Summa is a comprehensive philosophical examination, systematization, and defense of the worldview of Goatis, formerly known as Sv3rige. Jesse Anderson develops Goatis’s teachings into an extensive philosophical framework addressing the foundations of knowledge, the nature of reality, human existence, morality, health, society, and religion.

The work begins with epistemology and metaphysics: the relationship between sensory experience, reason, knowledge, existence, causation, and the natural world. It then develops an account of human nature and practical judgment, exploring instinct, survival, reproduction, moral nihilism, personal freedom, natural hierarchy, and the purposes of human life.

Later books address nutrition, raw animal foods, physical development, exercise, relationships, family, medicine, scientific authority, technology, political institutions, education, and modern civilization. Throughout, the Summa examines Goatis’s documented positions, including controversial historical claims, while distinguishing his recorded statements from the author’s independent philosophical arguments and the available empirical evidence.

The concluding four books investigate the existence of God. Drawing on classical and contemporary philosophy, they examine the cosmological arguments, Aquinas’s Five Ways, divine simplicity, contingency, the principle of sufficient reason, consciousness, moral arguments, fine-tuning, religious experience, miracles, divine hiddenness, and the problem of evil, and build a sustained case for naturalism and life without gods.

The method

How each Article proceeds

The Summa follows the form of classical scholastic philosophy. The aim is to investigate the foundations of each teaching and construct the strongest available arguments in its defense, not merely to summarize it.
