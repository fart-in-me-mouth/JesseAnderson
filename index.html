About

The Apexist Summa is a comprehensive philosophical examination, systematization, and defense of the worldview of Goatis, formerly known as Sv3rige. Across nineteen books, 165 formal Questions and more than 1,300 pages, it moves from knowledge and reality, through human nature, health and society, to an extended investigation of the existence of God.
